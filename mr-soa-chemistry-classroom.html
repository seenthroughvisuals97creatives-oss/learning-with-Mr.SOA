How This Classroom Works

Open to everyone, no registration needed: the periodic table, the Molar Mass Calculator, Mr. SOA's biography and books, the Notice Board, and the "Where Are You At?" self-check.

Only for registered students: the Practical Station, Resources, and the Google Classroom link — anything that leads to lessons, assignments or class materials.

To unlock those: register, and Mr. SOA will hand you a class access code. Enter it under "Join the Classroom" below — the system checks it for you. Every locked card still opens to explain itself; it simply tells you registration is needed instead of handing you the resource.
